Privacy Policy

App: IKnowMyMoney Expense Tracker • Effective Date: November 2, 2026
Core Privacy Promise: IKnowMyMoney is built on an offline-first architecture. All your accounts, transactions, budgets, and tags remain strictly on your local device. We run zero cloud servers, require no account sign-in, and never collect or sell your personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe personal financial records belong strictly to the individual. Therefore:

  • No Account Required: You never need to provide an email, phone number, name, or password to use IKnowMyMoney.
  • No Bank Account Linking: The App does not connect to bank accounts, Plaid, credit bureaus, or payment gateways. All transaction entries are logged manually by you.
  • No Network Data Harvesting: We do not track IP addresses, unique device identifiers (Android Advertising ID / IMEI), location data, or contact lists.

2. Local Device Storage

All data entered into IKnowMyMoney—including transaction amounts, category definitions, budget thresholds, tags, and accounts—is stored locally on your device within an encrypted application sandbox using SQLite.

This data is never automatically transmitted, synchronized to third-party clouds, or shared over the internet.

3. Application Permissions

IKnowMyMoney requests only the minimum Android permissions necessary to deliver local utilities:

  • Storage / Sharing Access: When you voluntarily choose to export transaction reports (CSV files) or generate database backup archives (.ikmm files), the App accesses the system share sheet or local file system to save your file.
  • Vibration / Haptics: Used solely to provide tactile keypad feedback when logging amounts.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

  • Zero Advertisements: IKnowMyMoney is 100% ad-free. No ad networks, ad tracking cookies, or banner SDKs are integrated into the application.
  • Zero Analytics / Telemetry SDKs: We do not integrate third-party tracking tools such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDK.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your financial records reside solely on your device, you have complete and exclusive control over data retention:

  • You may clear specific accounts, categories, or transaction histories directly inside the application settings.
  • Clearing application data via your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > IKnowMyMoney > Storage > Clear Storage) permanently deletes all records.
  • Uninstalling the application immediately removes the local SQLite database and all associated records from your phone.

6. Children's Privacy

IKnowMyMoney does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 18). Because the App does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date".
