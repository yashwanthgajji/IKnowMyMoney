## Differentiators: Built in IKnowMyMoney (Not in Original)
1. **Multiple Transaction Tags & Trip Budgets:** Assign multiple tags (trips, vendors, family members) to any transaction with dedicated target budgets and visual progress bars.
2. **Account Transaction Ledger Feed:** Tap any account to view its real-time calculated balance and a full transaction ledger in reverse chronological order.
3. **Historical Monthly Budget Navigation:** Browse past months' budgets with automatic monthly spending calculations.
4. **Interactive Date & Time Recording:** Quick 1-tap `[Today]` and `[Yesterday]` presets plus native Android calendar and clock pickers.
5. **Multi-line Notes with CSV Sanitization:** Enter detailed purchase notes with automatic newline escaping in CSV export.
6. **100% Offline SQLite Architecture:** High performance ACID transactions with WAL mode, zero accounts, zero tracking, zero telemetry.
7. **Instant Local Backup & Restore:** Export `.ikmm` database snapshots to any folder, messaging app, or cloud drive.
8. **Direct RFC 4180 CSV Export:** Clean export for Excel, Numbers, and Google Sheets with Tags and sanitization.
9. **Private Google Drive Sync Ready:** Direct cloud backup code prepared for user-owned Google Drive storage.
