## 2. Product Offering Tiers

### Tier 1: Personal (Current Release)
- **Price:** **$0 (100% Free Forever)**
- **Features Included:**
  - Unlimited transactions (Income, Expense, Transfer)
  - Unlimited accounts (Cash, Bank, Credit Cards)
  - Customizable categories with rich icons & color palettes
  - Category budget tracker with real-time threshold warnings
  - Visual monthly analytics and segmented charts
  - Global currency symbol switcher
  - Full CSV data export (RFC 4180 format)
  - Encrypted local database snapshot export (.ikmm)

### Tier 2: Supporter / Optional Tip (Future Roadmap)
- Optional voluntary tip jar ($1.99 / $4.99 one-time in-app purchase) for users who want to support ongoing open-source development.
- Zero locked features.
