## 2. The Problems We Solved
- **No Mandatory Cloud Accounts:** Most money apps force you to link bank accounts or sign up for cloud servers that sell your transaction data to advertisers. IKnowMyMoney requires no email, no password, and zero third-party cloud connections.
- **No Subscription Paywalls:** Core tracking features—unlimited accounts, custom categories, monthly budgets, and CSV export—are never locked behind monthly fees.
- **Instant Speed:** Loads immediately without loading spinners or internet lag.

## 3. How It Works (Three Simple Steps)
1. **Log in 3 Seconds:** Tap `+`, punch in the amount on the numeric keypad, pick a category, and save.
2. **Review Monthly Budgets:** Set targets for Groceries, Dining, or Bills. Color-coded progress indicators warn you before you overspend.
3. **Export Your Records Anytime:** Generate standard RFC 4180 CSV spreadsheets or portable `.ikmm` database snapshots to save anywhere you choose.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Where is my data stored?**  
**A:** Exclusively in your phone's internal storage sandbox. We operate zero servers and collect zero telemetry.

**Q: How do I backup my data?**  
**A:** Open Settings → Local Device Backup → Tap "Export Backup File (.ikmm)". You can save it to Google Drive, Files by Google, WhatsApp, or send it to your computer.

**Q: Can I open my transactions in Excel or Google Sheets?**  
**A:** Yes! Tap "Export Transactions CSV" in Settings to instantly create and share a clean `.csv` file formatted for Excel, Numbers, and Google Sheets.
