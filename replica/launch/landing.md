## 3. How It Works (Three Simple Steps)
1. **Log in 3 Seconds:** Tap `+`, punch in the amount, pick an account and category, and optionally add tags (e.g. "Costco", "LV Trip", "Me").
2. **Review Monthly & Trip Budgets:** Set targets for Groceries, Dining, or Vacation trips. Color-coded progress indicators warn you before you overspend.
3. **Export Your Records Anytime:** Generate standard RFC 4180 CSV spreadsheets or portable database snapshots to save anywhere you choose.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Where is my data stored?**  
**A:** Exclusively in your phone's internal storage sandbox. We operate zero servers and collect zero telemetry.

**Q: Can I track expenses from yesterday or a past trip?**  
**A:** Absolutely. When logging a transaction, tap "Yesterday" or tap the calendar/clock to select any past date and time.

**Q: Do category budgets reset monthly?**  
**A:** Yes! Category budgets automatically calculate your spending for the selected month. You can also use the month arrows (`◀` / `▶`) to review past months.

**Q: How do I backup my data?**  
**A:** Open Settings → Local Device Backup → Tap "Export Backup File (.ikmm)". You can save it to Google Drive, Files by Google, WhatsApp, or send it to your computer.

**Q: Can I open my transactions in Excel or Google Sheets?**  
**A:** Yes! Tap "Export Transactions CSV" in Settings to instantly create and share a clean `.csv` file formatted for Excel, Numbers, and Google Sheets.
