Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about your privacy and data ownership.

Where is my data stored?

Your transactions, accounts, and budgets are stored exclusively in your phone's internal application storage using an encrypted SQLite database. No external servers are contacted.

Can I open my transactions in Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. Tap "Export Transactions CSV" in Settings to instantly generate and share a clean spreadsheet file formatted for Excel, Numbers, and Google Sheets.

How do I back up my records?

In Settings, tap "Export Backup File (.ikmm)". You can save this snapshot to Google Drive, Files by Google, send it to your computer, or save it anywhere you trust.

Can I track expenses from past days or trips?

Yes! When recording a transaction, tap "Yesterday" or use the date/time picker to record purchases from any past date.
